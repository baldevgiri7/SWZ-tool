Using the SWZ Decision Support Tool

This interactive web application is designed to assist Arkansas Department of Transportation (ARDOT) personnel in making informed decisions about deploying various Smart Work Zone (SWZ) systems. By systematically evaluating project-specific conditions against predefined criteria, this tool helps quantify the need for different technologies.

Operational Guide:

  1. Understand Your Project: Before starting, ensure you have a thorough understanding of your project's scope, location, duration, traffic characteristics, known safety or operational issues, and geometric conditions.
  2. Evaluate Each SWZ System:
    • Use the navigation panel to select each SWZ system module.
    • Within each module, select the radio button that most accurately reflects the conditions of your specific project.
    • As you make selections, the scores will update in real-time.
  3. Review the Summary:
    • Navigate to the "Summary & Recommendations" page.
    • This page provides a consolidated table of scores and recommendations.
    • Use the "Generate PDF Summary" tool to create a formal report of your evaluation.
  4. Make an Informed Decision:
    • The scores and recommendations are intended to support your decision-making process.
    • Always combine these outputs with professional engineering judgment, ARDOT policies, and budget availability to arrive at the final decision.
